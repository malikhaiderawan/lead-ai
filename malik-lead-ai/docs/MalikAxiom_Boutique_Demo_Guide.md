## ⚡ Master Features List

1. **Brand Aesthetic Harmony**: Website ke buttons, badges, glows, borders aur text gradients client ke brand color ke sath dynamically change hote hain.
2. **Instagram Feed Integration (`#AsSeenOnInstagram`)**: 6 interactive grid posts with "Shop This Look", "Watch Reel", aur live Instagram handle matching!
3. **1-Click WhatsApp Ordering**: Har dress par pre-filled order message WhatsApp par transfer hota hai with dress title, size, price, and category.
4. **Interactive Shopping Bag Drawer**: Subtotal, 10% coupon (`EID10`), free delivery progress bar, and instant Cash on Delivery checkout.
5. **Cash on Delivery (COD) Slip Generator**: Automated slip generation sent to WhatsApp with Order ID `#RWJ-XXXXX`.
6. **Interactive Size Chart**: Sizing table in both **Inches** and **Centimeters (CM)** with unit toggle.
7. **Recent Sales Ticker (Social Proof)**: Subtle popups ("Ayesha from Lahore just ordered Noor-e-Jahan Luxury Pret via COD") jo conversion rate barhate hain.
8. **Sticky Mobile App Navigation**: Mobile view par native app ki tarah bottom bar (`Home`, `Catalog`, `Bag`, `WhatsApp Order`, `Theme`).
