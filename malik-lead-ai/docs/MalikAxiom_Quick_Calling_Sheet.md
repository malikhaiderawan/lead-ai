## 1. Staff / Receptionist Phone Uthaye

**Agent:**  
> *"Assalam-o-Alaikum! Main Haider baat kar raha hoon MalikAxiom Technology se. Mujhe [Business Name] ke owner ya marketing person se 30 seconds baat karni thi, online orders aur digital setup ke hawale se. Kya woh available hain?"*

* **Agar pooche: "Kis silsile mein?"**  
  > *"Actually unki Google listing/business presence dekhi thi. Ek quick digital growth opportunity discuss karni thi. Agar possible ho to call transfer kar dein, ya unka WhatsApp number mil sakta hai?"*

## 2. Owner Phone Uthaye

### Opening — 10 Seconds:
> *"Assalam-o-Alaikum Sir/Ma'am, Haider baat kar raha hoon MalikAxiom Technology se. Aapka [Business Name] Google/Instagram par dekha tha. Collection/business setup kaafi acha laga. Sir bas 30 seconds ki quick baat thi."*

### Phir (The Pitch):
> *"Hum local businesses ko online orders aur customer handling ke liye digital setup provide karte hain. Aapka current setup dekha to laga ke ek simple website/catalog aur direct WhatsApp ordering se customers ke liye ordering aur easy ho sakti hai."*

### CTA:
> *"Main aapko WhatsApp par ek 15-second ka demo bhej doon? Aap free time mein dekh lena, phir agar relevant laga to 2 minute baat kar lenge."*

* **Agar "haan" bole:**  
  > *"Perfect Sir. Yehi WhatsApp number hai na? Main abhi send karta hoon."*
* **Then (Follow-up lock):**  
  > *"Aur main kal [time] ke around ek quick follow-up kar loon?"*

## 🔥 7. Most Important Objection Replies

### 🔴 "Humein zaroorat nahi."
> *"Bilkul Sir, agar current setup smoothly chal raha hai to that's good. Main koi commitment nahi maang raha. Sirf ek 15-second sample dekh lein. Agar relevant lage to phir baat kar lenge."*

### 🔴 "WhatsApp par details bhej dein." *(Yeh GOOD sign hai!)*
> *"Bilkul Sir. Main abhi isi number par demo aur short portfolio bhej raha hoon. Aapka naam [Name] hi hai na? Main kal [time] par ek quick follow-up kar loon?"*  
> *(Note: Kabhi sirf 'okay' keh kar call disconnect mat karo. Follow-up time lock karo.)*

### 🔴 "Kitne ka hai?"
> *"Sir setup requirement par depend karta hai. Basic digital setup usually 10–20k range mein hota hai. Main pehle aapko relevant demo dikha deta hoon, phir exact requirement ke according price bata dunga."*

### 🔴 "Budget nahi hai."
> *"Samajh sakta hoon Sir. Isi liye hum small businesses ke liye simple packages rakhte hain. Pehle demo dekh lein, agar aapko value lage tabhi aage discuss karenge."*

### 🔴 "Already developer hai."
> *"Bohat achi baat hai Sir. Hum replace karne ke liye nahi keh rahe. Aap sirf hamara setup dekh lein. Agar koi useful feature lage to aap apne existing developer se bhi implement karwa sakte hain."*

### 🔴 "Busy hoon."
> *"Sure Sir, bilkul. Main WhatsApp par short demo bhej deta hoon. Aap bata dein kal 11 baje ya 3 baje kis time 2 minute ke liye convenient rahega?"*

### 🔴 "Interested nahi hoon."
> *"No issue Sir, completely understand. Main disturb nahi karunga. Agar future mein online ordering ya website ki requirement ho to aap hamara WhatsApp save kar sakte hain. Thank you for your time."* *(Respectfully exit)*

## 🎯 10. Call ka Golden Formula

```text
1. Salam + Introduction
   ↓
2. Business ka specific observation
   ↓
3. Growth opportunity
   ↓
4. Simple solution
   ↓
5. "WhatsApp par demo bhej doon?"
   ↓
6. Follow-up time lock
```

### ❌ Yeh Mat Karo:
* Company ki 2-minute lambi history sunana
* Apni skills ki long list batana
* *"Hum best agency hain"* ke naarey lagana
* Bohat technical terms use karna
* Customer ko force karna ya argue karna
* Fake statistics bolna
* Pehli call par detailed complex proposal bhejna

### ✅ Yeh Karo:
* Sirf customer ke business ki baat
* Customer ke direct benefit ki baat
* Short sentences & active listening
* Natural Urdu-English mix
* Demo link WhatsApp par drop karna
* Follow-up appointment ka time lock karna
