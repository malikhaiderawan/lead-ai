## 🧠 Chapter 1: The Caller's Mindset & Preparation (Call Se Pehle Ki Tayari)

### 1. The Core Objective
> **Cold call par direct sale close nahi karni.**  
> Call ka wahid maqsad (primary goal) customer ka **30-second attention grab karna** aur unse **WhatsApp par 15-second demo video / portfolio link** dekhne ki permission lena hai.

### 2. Pre-Call Dashboard Reconnaissance (M7 Dashboard Check)
Har call milane se pehle M7 Cold Calling Station par yeh 4 cheezein zaroor dekhein:
1. **Business Name & Category:** E.g., Anmol Boutique (Clothing) ya Ali Dental Clinic (Healthcare).
2. **Location / City:** Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, etc.
3. **Google Rating & Reviews:** E.g., 4.8 stars (78 reviews) — isko compliment ke liye use karein.
4. **Current Digital State:** Website mojood hai ya missing hai? Social media active hai ya nahi?

### 3. Terminology & Tone Rules
* **Golden Rule #1 (Positive Language):** Kabhi bhi customer ko yeh na kahein ke *"Aapke business mein problem hai"* ya *"Aapka kaam theek nahi chal raha"*. Hamesha **"Growth Opportunity"**, **"Digital Setup"**, ya **"Next-Level Automation"** ka lafz use karein.
* **Golden Rule #2 (Energy & Pacing):** Pehle 3 seconds mein aapki energy positive, confident aur warm honi chahiye. Awaz mein hichkichahat (hesitation) na ho.
* **Golden Rule #3 (Language):** Natural Roman Urdu / conversational Urdu-English mix use karein. Bohat zyada heavy technical English terms bol kar client ko confuse na karein.

## 📊 Chapter 6: Pipeline Status Updating Guidelines (M7 Dashboard)

Har call ke baad M7 Dashboard par status lazmi update karein:
1. **CONTACT FIRST:** Agar customer ne genuine interest dikhaya ya WhatsApp demo mangwaya.

## ✅ Chapter 7: Daily Caller Dos & Don'ts Checklist

### 10 Crucial DOs:
1. Call milane se pehle business ka naam aur category yaad kar lein.
2. Call shuru hote hi polite smile ke sath bolein (awaz mein energy aati hai).
3. Hamesha customer ka good name confirm karein.
4. Call ko 60 seconds ke andar deliver karein taake customer bore na ho.
5. Har call ke foran baad WhatsApp message send karein.
6. Har objection ko calm reh kar acknowledge karein ("Bilkul sahi kaha aapne...").
7. Price poochnay par transparent rahein (PKR 10,000 – 20,000).
8. Specific follow-up time lock karein (e.g. "Kal dopahar 3 baje").
9. M7 Dashboard par call notes time par likhein.
10. Target daily: Minimum 40–50 calls complete karein.

### 10 Strict DON'Ts:
1. Kabhi customer se behes (argue) mat karein.
2. "Aapka business khrab chal raha hai" jaisa negative word mat bolein.
3. Call par 5 minute ka lamba technical lecture mat dein.
4. Unrealistic waday (e.g. "Kal subah hi 100 orders aa jayenge") mat karein.
5. Agar customer mana kare to rude mat hon, polite note par end karein.
6. WhatsApp bheje bagair lead ko complete mat samjhein.
7. Landline number par WhatsApp pitch mat karein.
8. Call background mein shor (traffic/noise) na aane dein.
9. "Aapko kisne number diya?" par panic mat hon; bolen: "Aapki public Google business listing se mila".
10. Kabhi daily quota drop na hone dein.
