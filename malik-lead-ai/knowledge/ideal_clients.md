# Ideal Clients — MalikAxiom Lead AI

> Internal knowledge base file for **MalikAxiom Lead AI**.
> Defines who MalikAxiom serves and what an ideal lead looks like.

## Who MalikAxiom Serves

**Business types:**

- Startups
- Entrepreneurs
- SMEs
- Small businesses
- Growing businesses

**Geography:** Pakistan and internationally.

## Digital Maturity Bands (For Research)

Assess how digitally mature a business *appears* from observable evidence only:

| Band          | Typical observations                                                                 |
|---------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| None          | No findable digital presence at all                                                  |
| Minimal       | Personal/unclear social account used as the business presence; no website            |
| Basic         | Some social pages and/or a free/basic site; incomplete profiles; inconsistent content|
| Established   | Professional website, complete Google Business Profile, active socials               |
| Strong        | Website + SEO + complete profiles + consistent content and engagement                |

> These bands describe **observed presence only** — never infer revenue, customer volume,
> or technology stack from them.

## High-Potential Client Profiles

### Profile A — Offline / raw business (→ Business Digitalization)

Real, active business with little or no professional digital presence.
May have a phone number, word-of-mouth, or an informal social account only.

### Profile B — Social seller without a store (→ E-Commerce / Shopify)

Sells products through social media (e.g., Instagram/Facebook DMs) but has no
proper online store, catalog, or ordering system.

### Profile C — Outdated digital presence (→ Web Development / Revamp)

Has a website, but it is outdated, non-responsive, or provides a weak digital
experience relative to what the business now offers.

### Profile D — Process-heavy manual operations (→ Business Automation)

Repetitive tasks, manual customer/order handling, or repeated data entry that
could be automated; existing tools that do not talk to each other.

### Profile E — Established business with app use case (→ Mobile Development)

Legitimate, established business with a strong customer-facing reason for a
mobile app (not an app idea without a business behind it).

### Profile F — Clear AI use case (→ AI Integration)

An observable workflow or product where AI would genuinely add value —
not AI suggested merely because it is trendy.

## Poor Fit / Likely Skip

- Irrelevant businesses outside MalikAxiom's service scope
- Duplicates of leads already captured
- Inactive, closed, or abandoned businesses
- Unverifiable businesses (no way to confirm they exist/operate)
- Businesses with a strong, healthy digital presence and no clear gap to address

These map to the **SKIP** category in [qualification_rules.md](qualification_rules.md).
