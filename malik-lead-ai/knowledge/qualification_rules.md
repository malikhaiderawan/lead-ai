# Qualification Rules — MalikAxiom Lead AI

> Internal knowledge base file for **MalikAxiom Lead AI**.
> Defines the research philosophy, scoring categories, and evidence-based service matching.

---

## 1. Lead Research Philosophy

The future AI agent must **NOT** simply identify whether a business has a website.
It must determine:

1. What kind of business this is.
2. How digitally mature the business appears.
3. What digital presence already exists.
4. What important digital gaps can be factually observed.
5. Which MalikAxiom services are genuinely relevant.
6. Which service should be the primary offer.
7. Which additional services could potentially be offered.
8. Why this business qualifies as a lead.
9. How strong the sales opportunity appears.
10. How MalikAxiom should approach the business.

## 2. Evidence Classes

Every research statement must be classified as one of:

| Class                  | Meaning                                                        |
|------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Verified fact**      | Directly observed/retrieved from a source (site, profile, listing) |
| **Reasonable opportunity** | An inference clearly grounded in verified facts               |
| **Unknown information**| Could not be determined — must be marked unknown, not guessed  |

### Never Invent

Never invent a:

- business problem (challenge)
- revenue figure
- technology stack
- customer volume
- owner identity
- business need

If it was not observed, it is **unknown**.

---

## 3. Sales Potential Categories

### HIGH

Clear digital opportunity, active/genuine business, and strong MalikAxiom service fit.

### MODERATE

Potential opportunity exists but urgency, need, or fit is not sufficiently clear.

### LOW

Business is legitimate but there is little obvious opportunity, or the business
already has a relatively strong digital presence.

### SKIP

Irrelevant, duplicate, inactive, unverifiable, or clearly unsuitable lead.

### Reasoning Requirement

**The system must explain the reasoning behind every category.**
A category without evidence-based reasoning is invalid.

---

## 4. Special Requirement — "Why This Lead"

Every qualified lead must eventually answer:

> **"Why did MalikAxiom Lead AI show me this lead?"**

The answer must be based on **actual researched evidence**.

### Acceptable Example

> "This business appears active, has an existing social presence, but lacks a
> professional website and broader digital presence. MalikAxiom's 20K Business
> Digitalization package directly addresses several of these observed gaps."

### Unacceptable (Generic) Example

> "They need a website because every business needs one."

Generic reasoning is forbidden.

---

## 5. Service Matching (Evidence-Based)

Match services based on evidence — never merely because a service exists in
[services.md](services.md).

| Observed evidence                                    | Primary service candidate      |
|------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Little/no digital presence                          | Business Digitalization — 20K  |
| Products sold through social media, no proper store | E-Commerce / Shopify           |
| Existing website but outdated/weak digital experience | Web Development / Website Revamp |
| Manual repetitive business processes                | Business Automation            |
| Established business with strong customer-facing app use case | Mobile Development    |
| Relevant AI use case                                | AI Integration                 |

**Rules:**

- Name one **primary offer** per lead; additional services must be listed separately
  as *potential* follow-on offers.
- Every recommended service must cite the evidence that justifies it.
- If no service can be justified by evidence, the lead cannot be HIGH.
