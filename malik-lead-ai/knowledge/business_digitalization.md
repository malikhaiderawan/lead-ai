# Business Digitalization — 20,000 PKR Package

> Internal knowledge base file for **MalikAxiom Lead AI**.
> Detailed reference for MalikAxiom's core digitalization package.

## What It Is

A core service package for small/offline businesses that have **little or no professional
digital presence**.

**Positioning:** Take a normal/raw/offline business and establish a professional digital presence.

**Price:** 20,000 PKR.

## Package Can Include

- Responsive business website
- Landing page where appropriate
- Google Business Profile setup
- Google Maps presence/setup
- Google Search Console setup
- Basic SEO setup
- Instagram setup
- Facebook setup
- TikTok setup
- 5 branded social media posts
- 3 reels
- Premium basic branding/presentation

> Not every item is mandatory for every lead — the exact combination should be shaped by
> the observed gaps of the specific business.

## Intended Benefits (Allowed Claims)

- Improved online visibility
- Professional appearance
- Customer discovery
- A stronger digital foundation

## Claims Rules (Hard Constraints)

- **Do NOT guarantee revenue or profit** — in research notes or in outreach.
- Do not promise specific rankings, follower counts, or sales outcomes.
- Only claim the intended benefits listed above.

## When This Package Is the Right Fit

Strong fit when observed evidence shows a business that is:

- Active and genuine (real operations, not a dead listing), AND
- Has little or no professional digital presence — e.g., no website, no complete
  Google Business Profile, missing or inconsistent social accounts, AND
- Would benefit from a complete foundational setup rather than a single isolated asset.

Weak fit when the business already has a strong, professional digital presence —
in that case, consider other services from [services.md](services.md) or classify
per [qualification_rules.md](qualification_rules.md).
