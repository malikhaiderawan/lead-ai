## 3. Milestone 2 (M2) — Deep Research & Digital Footprint Intelligence Engine

The **M2 Research Engine** inspects raw inputs (business name, raw phone, address, social handles) and performs deep, multi-source digital footprint verification without manual human browsing.

### 3.1 Research Modules
* **Input Loader (`input_loader.py`):** Ingests raw CSV exports from Google Maps Scrapers, Instagram scrapers, or manual lists; normalizes phone numbers and cleans business names.
* **Website Researcher (`website_researcher.py`):**
  * HTTP/HTTPS availability probe.
  * SSL Certificate verification.
  * Mobile responsiveness detection.
  * E-commerce platform fingerprinting (Shopify, WooCommerce, Custom, None).
  * WhatsApp ordering integration check (detects `wa.me` links or automated checkout).
* **Social Media Researcher (`social_researcher.py`):**
  * Audits Instagram presence (handle validity, follower count, posting frequency, engagement signals).
  * Audits Facebook & TikTok presence.
* **Google Presence Auditor (`google_researcher.py`):**
  * Google Business Profile (GBP) claimed/unclaimed status.
  * Google Maps review score and rating count.
* **Evidence Synthesizer (`evidence.py`):**
  * Extracts concrete, factual evidence points used later to write ultra-personalized outreach.

### 3.2 Output Artifacts
* `output/research_results.json` — Deep structured evidence tree.
* `output/research_results.csv` — Tabular research audit for spreadsheet review.

## 4. Milestone 3 (M3) — Rule-Based Lead Qualification & Service Matching Algorithm

The **M3 Engine** applies deterministic, evidence-based business logic to qualify leads and rank their sales readiness.

### 4.1 Qualification Rules & Scoring Formula

$$\text{Total Score} = S_{\text{Presence}} + S_{\text{Maturity}} + S_{\text{Fit}} + S_{\text{Actionability}} - P_{\text{Risk}}$$

1. **Digital Presence Score (0–30 pts):**
   * Has active Instagram with >1,000 followers: `+15 pts`
   * Has Google Maps listing with positive reviews: `+10 pts`
   * Has verifiable business address: `+5 pts`
2. **Opportunity / Challenge Score (0–40 pts):**
   * High social activity but **NO website**: `+30 pts` (Highest conversion opportunity)
   * Has slow/broken non-mobile website: `+20 pts`
   * Taking manual orders via DMs with no automated cart/WhatsApp slip: `+20 pts`
3. **Contact Actionability Score (0–30 pts):**
   * Valid Pakistani mobile/WhatsApp number (+92 3XX XXXXXXX): `+30 pts`
   * Landline only or unverified number: `0 pts` (Flagged for manual phone lookup)

### 4.2 Priority Decision Gating

```

├───────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
```

## 5. Milestone 4 (M4) — Personalized Multi-Channel Outreach Composer

The **M4 Composer** constructs bespoke outreach messages using the proven **Observation → Opportunity → Solution → Soft CTA** framework.

### 5.1 Outreach Message Anatomy
Every outreach message generated by M4 adheres to strict psychological conversion guidelines:
1. **Personalized Hook (Observation):** Mentions a specific, real observation (e.g., *"I was exploring your gorgeous bridal velvet collection on Instagram..."*).
2. **Identified Opportunity (Challenge):** Points out a friction point respectfully (e.g., *"Noticed customers currently have to ask for prices and sizes manually in DMs, which leads to lost sales during busy hours."*).
3. **MalikAxiom Value Proposition (Solution):** Presents the tailored storefront demo (e.g., *"We've built a live luxury demo store where clients can view sizes, pick colors, and place 1-Click WhatsApp orders with Cash on Delivery."*).
4. **Frictionless Next Step (Soft CTA):** Low-pressure call to action (e.g., *"Would you like me to share a 60-second preview link configured with your brand name?"*).

### 5.2 Multi-Channel Output Formats
* **WhatsApp Direct Message** (Concise, formatted with emojis, max 150 words).
* **Instagram Direct Message (DM)** (Short, informal, punchy, curiosity-driven).
* **Cold Email** (Executive summary with audit bullet points).

## 6. Milestone 5 (M5) — Lead Pipeline Synthesis, Audit & Master Sales Reporting

The **M5 Module** joins all data streams (M2 Research + M3 Qualification + M4 Outreach) into unified master deliverables for the sales team.

### 6.1 Data Synthesis & Quality Auditing (`auditor.py`, `merger.py`)
* **Deduplication:** Normalizes phone numbers (+92 format) and business names to prevent duplicate outreach.
* **Audit Flags:** Identifies anomalies (e.g., `MISSING_PHONE`, `LANDLINE_ONLY`, `NO_SOCIAL_LINK`, `DUPLICATE_NAME`).
* **Sales Segmentation:** Splits leads into actionable batch spreadsheets.

### 6.2 Key M5 Deliverables
1. `output/malikaxiom_sales_leads.csv` — Master CRM CSV containing all 45+ columns (metrics, scores, messages, phone, social, audit flags).
2. `output/contact_first.csv` — Curated priority CSV containing only vetted `CONTACT FIRST` leads ready for immediate closing.
3. `output/malikaxiom_sales_summary.md` — Human-readable executive briefing detailing pipeline metrics, category breakdowns, and top targets.

## 7. Milestone 6 (M6) — WhatsApp Outreach Execution & Safety Dispatch Engine

The **M6 Execution Engine** delivers the first outreach message safely and reliably while strictly enforcing anti-spam and duplicate-send protection.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        M6 SAFETY DISPATCH PIPELINE                     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Eligibility Gate       → Verifies lead is M5-approved 'CONTACT FIRST'│
│ 2. Phone Validator        → Normalizes to Pakistani mobile (+923XXXXXXXXX)│
│ 3. Send Tracker Check     → Inspects m6_send_state.json for duplicates │
│ 4. Message Validator      → Ensures no placeholder or broken links     │
│ 5. Confirmation Step      → Requires explicit human confirmation       │
│ 6. Channel Execution      → Dispatches via WhatsApp (wa.me / Playwright│
│ 7. State & Log Record     → Atomic append to state JSON & outreach log │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 7.1 Safety Guarantees & Duplicate Protection
* **Persistent State File (`output/m6_send_state.json`):** Tracks every attempted lead ID, timestamp, channel, and status (`SENT`, `FAILED`, `NOT_READY`, `NO_WHATSAPP_NUMBER`). Once marked `SENT`, a lead is permanently blocked from duplicate sends.
* **Dry-Run Mode (`--dry-run`):** Allows full inspection of proposed messages, recipients, and channels without sending any packets or modifying state.
* **Safety Confirmation Gate:** In live mode, the CLI prompts the operator with full message details and requires typing `SEND` to authorize.
* **Dual Dispatch Channels:**
  1. `whatsapp.py` (Default Assist Mode): Generates official `https://wa.me/` direct click-to-chat links with URL-encoded messages for rapid one-click operator sending.
  2. `whatsapp_playwright.py` (Headless Automation): Optional automated browser session for controlled batch outreach.

## 🔒 Key Developer Rules for Future Agents on New Laptop

1. **Zero Unnecessary Refactoring:** Do **NOT** rewrite the storefronts in React, Vue, Next.js, or Vite. They are intentionally built as single-file, zero-dependency static HTML/Tailwind/JS apps for maximum speed, instant client customization, and 100% Vercel compatibility.
2. **Dynamic Centralization Rule:** Never hardcode client brand names. Always configure `brandConfig.name` and `brandConfig.title` in Demo 5.
3. **Demo 2 Multi-File Mirror Integrity:** Always maintain parity across all 6 Demo 2 mirror files.
4. **Terminology Compliance:** Always use **"challenges"** and **"growth opportunities"** — never *"problems"*.
